Lucky Sic Bo Analyzer Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lucky Sic Bo Analyzer (the “App”), provided by SungHoon Lee (이성훈, the “Developer”), handles information. It applies to the Android App, its Google Play PRO subscription verification service, and support communications.

Effective date: July 15, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Service scope and important notice

The App is an informational and entertainment analysis tool. It does not accept or transmit wagers, hold user funds, process casino deposits or withdrawals, or connect users to a gambling operator. Its nine named models use independent fair three-dice samples. The names are display labels and do not represent different predictive algorithms.

Dice outcomes are independent. Past results, model samples, statistics, highlighted cells, or other App output do not change real-world dice probabilities, guarantee a result, or provide financial, investment, gambling, or legal advice. Users remain responsible for their decisions, losses, and compliance with the laws and age restrictions that apply to them.

2. Information kept on the device

The App may keep the following information only on the device:

  • dice inputs, current session history, and analyzer state;
  • display, language, vibration, and consent preferences;
  • a cryptographically random App installation identifier; and
  • an encrypted, server-signed, short-lived PRO entitlement.

Dice inputs, analyzer history, statistics, and guide results are not sent to the Developer’s subscription server. Local information can be cleared using App controls where available, by clearing App storage, or by uninstalling the App, subject to Android backup and device settings. Secure PRO credentials are intended to be excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

3. Information processed for Google Play PRO

When a user purchases, restores, or rechecks PRO, the following limited information is transmitted over HTTPS to the Developer’s verification service:

  • Google Play purchase token;
  • random App installation identifier;
  • package, product, base-plan, and offer identifiers where applicable;
  • request time, random nonce, and App version;
  • Play Integrity token; and
  • subscription state returned by Google Play, including expiry, acknowledgement, renewal, cancellation, replacement, refund, revocation, grace, hold, or pause status where applicable.

Raw purchase and Integrity tokens are redacted from application logs. Purchase and installation identifiers are converted to domain-separated one-way hashes for routine lookup. A raw purchase token may be stored only when continuing verification or reconciliation requires it, and then only encrypted at rest using managed key protection. A raw Integrity token is not retained after the one-time verification request.

The Developer does not receive a user’s card number, bank account, Google password, Google account email, billing address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone or camera content, advertising identifier, or local analyzer history through the verification service.

4. Technical and support information

The server and its hosting provider may process limited technical records such as an IP address or short-lived keyed IP hash, user agent, request identifier, request and response time, response status, App version, rate-limit state, security result, and service diagnostic. These records are used only to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and prevent abuse of subscription verification.

If a user emails the Developer, the Developer receives the sender’s email address and message contents. Support information is used to answer the request, investigate a problem, protect legal rights, or meet a legal obligation. Users should not include payment-card data, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information in support email.

5. Purposes and lawful handling

The Developer processes the limited information above to:

  • verify, grant, restore, refresh, and revoke PRO access;
  • confirm package, product, purchase, and App integrity;
  • acknowledge purchases and respond to Google Play notifications;
  • detect replay, token sharing, fraud, and unauthorized access;
  • maintain availability, diagnose errors, and answer support requests; and
  • comply with law and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Depending on the user’s jurisdiction, processing is based on performing the subscription service requested by the user, compliance with law, the Developer’s overriding legitimate interest in security and fraud prevention, or consent where consent is legally required. The App does not sell personal information, use it for behavioral advertising, or perform cross-app tracking.

6. Service providers and disclosure

  • Google Play: billing, purchase status, subscription management, refund/revocation information, Real-time Developer Notifications, and Play Integrity.
  • Google Cloud: verification API hosting, database, managed signing/key services, authenticated notification delivery, security controls, and limited service logging.
  • GitHub Pages: public hosting of this policy. GitHub may receive ordinary web request information when this page is visited; GitHub does not receive App analyzer history from the App.

These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy documentation. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with a binding legal request, protect users or the service, investigate abuse, or complete a business transfer subject to legally required notice and safeguards. The Developer does not disclose App data to casinos, betting operators, data brokers, or advertising networks.

8. Retention, deletion, and subscription cancellation

  • Raw Integrity tokens and request nonces are used for a single verification and are not intentionally retained afterward.
  • Developer-controlled purchase/installation hashes, encrypted purchase reference where required, entitlement state, binding, verification/security records, and RTDN receipts are assigned an automatic deletion deadline no later than 90 days after creation. A later check does not extend the original record’s deadline. If a deleted subscription is verified again, a new limited record may be created with a new deadline of no more than 90 days.
  • Short-lived rate-limit and access records are retained only as long as operationally necessary and no longer than 90 days unless a specific security incident or law requires restricted retention.
  • Support email is retained until the request is resolved and may be retained longer only when reasonably necessary for dispute, security, fraud-prevention, or legal-record purposes.
  • Information subject to a legal hold, binding order, refund dispute, chargeback, fraud investigation, or mandatory recordkeeping may be retained for the required period, isolated from ordinary use, then deleted or irreversibly de-identified.

The App does not create a Developer account. Users may request deletion of Developer-controlled installation and subscription- verification data through the in-App deletion control when released, or by emailing digitalspaceout.official@gmail.com. The Developer may request limited proof needed to locate the relevant anonymous record and prevent unauthorized deletion. Requests are handled without unreasonable delay, subject to applicable law and the limited exceptions above.

Deleting App data or verification records does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription separately in Google Play. Conversely, cancelling a subscription stops future renewal but does not itself delete local App data or records that remain within the retention period.

9. User rights and automated verification

Subject to applicable law, a user may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent where relied upon, or information about processing. Because the service is account-free and identifiers are pseudonymous, the Developer may be unable to locate a record from an email address alone.

Entitlement decisions are made automatically from Google Play state, signed requests, and Integrity/security checks. A failed check may keep PRO locked or end a short offline entitlement. A user who believes the result is wrong may request manual support review. No automated decision is used to set gambling odds, personalize ads, or build a behavioral profile.

Planned safeguards include HTTPS, signed short-lived entitlements, Android Keystore-backed storage, managed signing keys, encryption of any retained raw purchase reference, one-way domain-separated hashes, least-privilege service identities, package/product allow-lists, Play Integrity, replay protection, rate limits, restricted database access, log redaction, TTL deletion, and separate production and test environments.

No transmission or storage system is completely secure. The Developer does not promise absolute security and is not responsible for compromise caused solely by a user’s device, operating system, Google account, network, or conduct, to the extent permitted by law. This does not exclude rights or liability that cannot legally be waived. Legally required security incidents will be investigated and notified to affected users or authorities as required.

The App is not directed to children and is intended only for adults who meet the age requirements applicable in their location. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from a child through the App. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent information directly to the Developer may request its deletion.
