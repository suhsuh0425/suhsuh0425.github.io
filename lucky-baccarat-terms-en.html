2. Analysis and Scoring

The App is provided for entertainment and reference. Each panel result is generated randomly using fixed probabilities configured in the App. Entered results are displayed on the scoreboards and used to calculate panel performance. They are not used to generate subsequent panel results. The displayed “win rate” is the proportion of counted rounds in which the panel result matches the entered result.

  • Android. A round is excluded from scoring when only one of the panel result and the entered result is a tie. A round is counted when both results are ties or neither is a tie.
  • iOS. Any round with a generated panel result and a result entered by the user is counted, including rounds with ties.

Displayed figures do not represent actual betting win rates or guarantee future game outcomes, accuracy or profit. The App does not accept bets, pay winnings, process deposits or withdrawals, exchange currency, connect to casinos or act as an intermediary for real-money betting. The information in the App does not constitute financial, investment or betting advice.
