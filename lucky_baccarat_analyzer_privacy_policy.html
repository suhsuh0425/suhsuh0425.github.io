Lucky Baccarat Analyzer Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Lucky Baccarat Analyzer, an offline baccarat analysis app provided by SungHoon Lee (이성훈).

Effective date: May 5, 2026
1.

Summary

Lucky Baccarat Analyzer is designed to work offline. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information from within the App for its core analyzer experience.

The App does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising identifiers, or web views for the analyzer feature.

Important Notice

The App is provided for informational and entertainment purposes only. It does not provide gambling services, accept wagers, or facilitate real-money betting.

Any analysis, predictions, or statistics shown in the App do not guarantee outcomes and must not be treated as financial, betting, or gambling advice. Users are responsible for complying with the laws and regulations that apply in their location.

2.

Information Stored on Your Device

To remember your preferences, the App stores only the following settings locally on your device:

  • dark mode preference
  • vibration preference
  • language preference
  • startup consent status

These settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to the Developer from within the App.

3.

Information the App Does Not Collect

The App does not collect personal information from within the App such as:

  • name, email address, or phone number
  • precise or approximate location
  • contacts, photos, microphone input, or camera data
  • payment information or financial account information
  • device advertising identifiers
  • usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
4.

Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The App is intended to operate offline and does not rely on an internet connection for its analyzer features.

The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

If your device or operating system automatically backs up app data, that backup is handled by your platform provider under its own terms and settings, not by the Developer.

App stores or platform providers such as Google Play or Apple may process information related to downloading, updating, billing, or platform operations under their own privacy policies. Those activities are outside the App's direct operation.

5.

Contacting the Developer

If you contact the Developer by email, the Developer will receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

That information will be used only to respond to your inquiry or address your request, unless disclosure is required by law.

6.

Data Retention and Deletion

Preference settings stored locally on your device remain there until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, subject to any device-level backup settings you choose to use.

Because the Developer does not receive these locally stored settings from within the App, the Developer cannot independently access or delete them on your device.

7.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the Developer by email, please contact the Developer so appropriate steps can be taken.

8.

Security

Because the App is designed to work offline and does not transmit personal data from within the App for its core analyzer experience, the Developer does not maintain server-side personal data for normal App operation.

The security of locally stored settings depends in part on the security controls of your device and operating system.

9.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If it is updated, the revised version will be posted with a new effective date.
