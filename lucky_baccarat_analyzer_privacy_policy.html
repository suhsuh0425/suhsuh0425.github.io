This Privacy Policy applies to Lucky Baccarat Analyzer (the App), which SH Software, represented by Sunghoon Lee (the Operator), makes available on Google Play and the App Store.

You can use the basic analysis features without an account. The App processes entered game results and analysis history on your device. The verification server processes information needed to provide paid features, verify purchases and protect the service. Customer support is handled by email.

1. Information Processed on Your Device

  • Analysis records. On Android, the App processes entered game results and analysis history in memory while it runs. On iOS, the App stores game results, panel results and in-progress analysis on the device so you can resume after relaunching the App. A recovery copy may remain on the device when the App attempts to recover corrupted records on iOS. The App does not send analysis inputs or history to the Operator's server.
  • Preferences. The App stores preferences on the device, such as language, display mode, vibration and whether you have acknowledged the introductory notice.
  • Purchase information. The App may keep a randomly generated installation identifier, signed entitlements issued after verification and proofs needed to verify purchases or recover incomplete transactions in protected device storage.

2. Data Processed by the Server and Customer Support and How It Is Used

Providing paid features and verifying purchases
When you purchase, restore or reverify PRO, or when you purchase or reverify an entitlement for a time-limited cosmetic item or handle an incomplete transaction, the Operator processes the store and product identifiers, a randomly generated App installation identifier and the request time. The Operator also processes the request type, App version and build number if included in the request. On Android, the Operator processes Google Play purchase tokens and Play Integrity verification information. On iOS, the Operator processes transaction proofs signed by the App Store. To verify purchases, prevent duplicate grants and reflect subscriptions, cancellations and refunds, the Operator checks purchase status, subscription expiry, cosmetic grant, expiry and refund status, and information associated with store notifications.
Verification records
The server ledger records references hashed from purchase or transaction identifiers and App installation identifiers, purchase and subscription status, verification results and information needed for cosmetic grants and refunds. Hashed references may still be linked to purchase or installation records. A separate record used to prevent duplicate grants of Apple consumable cosmetic items contains only the hashed transaction reference and a flag indicating that the item was granted.
Service operations and security
The Operator may process technical logs containing information such as IP addresses, user agents, request times and paths, response status and error types to diagnose errors, limit access, prevent misuse and respond to security issues. The App does not request device location permission or use dedicated SDKs for advertising, behavioral analytics or crash reporting. The Operator does not use purchase or technical records for personalized advertising or cross-app tracking.
Customer support and exercising your rights
For support requests received by email, the Operator processes the reply address, the request, error information supplied by the user and the minimum information needed to verify a purchase. Google or Apple account passwords, full card numbers, original purchase tokens and signed transaction proofs should not be sent.

The Operator processes personal information as needed to enter into or perform the service contract, including purchase verification and entitlement restoration, or to comply with legal obligations. To prevent misuse and protect the service, the Operator processes relevant information to a reasonable extent only where applicable law allows it and its legitimate interests clearly outweigh the rights of the individuals concerned. Where consent is required, the Operator provides the necessary information and obtains consent before processing. Without the information needed to verify a purchase, verification or restoration of a paid entitlement may be limited.

4. Retention and Disposal

Analysis records, preferences and entitlements on the device
On Android, analysis records are retained while the App runs. On iOS, you can clear current analysis records with the App’s reset feature. Clearing App data or removing the App may delete preferences and recovery copies stored on the device. An operating system backup may restore some data. The App’s backup exclusion rules apply to protected purchase information on Android. Depending on operating system behavior, iOS Keychain items may remain after reinstalling the App. Signed entitlements cannot unlock paid features after they expire.
Subscription verification and store notification receipt records
Subscription verification records and notification receipt records are scheduled for automatic deletion after 90 days, measured from initial creation and receipt, respectively. Rechecking an existing subscription record alone does not extend its retention period. If a purchase is verified after the original record is deleted, a new record may be created as needed.
Detailed purchase and installation records for time-limited cosmetics
Pending purchase reservation records become eligible for automatic deletion 90 days after initial creation. Detailed cosmetic grant records become eligible for automatic deletion 90 days after the later of the processing time and the recorded cosmetic expiry. An additional purchase, refund or change in the record’s status may change that reference time. Rechecking a valid cosmetic item does not extend retention if its status has not changed.
Apple cosmetic duplicate-grant prevention records
The Operator retains the hashed transaction reference and fulfillment flag only as long as necessary to prevent duplicate grants. These records may be kept after the cosmetic benefit expires to prevent the same purchase from being granted again. The Operator deletes them when they are no longer needed for this purpose. They do not contain original purchase proofs, installation identifiers, product names, purchase amounts or transaction times. The 90-day automatic deletion schedule for detailed records does not apply to these records.
Technical logs and cloud audit logs
General technical logs are configured for 30 days of retention. Required audit logs, such as records of cloud administration, are held in a separate bucket configured for 400 days of retention.
Customer support and statutory retention
The Operator retains general inquiries until their purpose has been met, including any reply and necessary follow-up. Once an inquiry is closed, the Operator reviews the need for retention case by case and manually deletes messages and attachments with no remaining basis for retention. Messages in Gmail Trash can be recovered for up to 30 days, after which Gmail deletes them automatically. If the law requires earlier disposal, the Operator deletes those messages from Trash as well. Gmail deletion instructions
Each store manages its own order and payment records. Where the Operator holds records subject to retention under Korean e-commerce law, contract, withdrawal, payment and supply records are retained for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints and dispute handling for 3 years. Personal information retained under a legal obligation is stored and managed separately from other personal information. If there is a basis to retain records for an ongoing refund dispute, misuse investigation or legal claim, the Operator restricts access and limits retention to the records necessary for that purpose. The Operator disposes of those records once the basis for retention ends.

The time at which server records become eligible for automatic deletion may differ from the time deletion completes. Once the processing purpose has been met or the retention period has expired, electronic records are disposed of irreversibly unless retention is required by law.

5. User Rights and Automated Verification Inquiries

Google Play and App Store users can also consult the data deletion instructions on the public support page for the request process and retention practices.

Users or their authorized representatives may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent in accordance with applicable law. Requests to delete server records can be submitted to digitalspaceout.official@gmail.com. Specify which records should be deleted and which store you used. If needed to verify a purchase record, the Operator may request the purchase date, product name or order number. Do not send passwords, full card numbers, original purchase tokens or signed transaction proofs.

The Operator verifies whether the requester is the individual whose data is involved or an authorized representative, then compares the submitted information with transaction information available from the store. The Operator deletes records in its possession that can be identified as belonging to the requester and that meet the conditions for deletion. An email address alone may not be enough to identify purchase or installation records. For App Store purchases, the Operator checks whether available transaction information can identify the corresponding server record. Apple cosmetic duplicate-grant prevention records contain only a hashed transaction reference and a grant flag, so the Operator checks whether the available information can be matched to the transaction reference.

For correction or deletion requests under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, the Operator provides notice of the outcome within 10 days of receipt. If deletion is restricted by law or the requested record cannot be identified, the Operator explains the affected scope, the reason and how to raise an objection. For technical and audit logs, the Operator checks whether the requester’s records can be isolated and identified. Logs past their retention period are deleted under the settings described in Retention and Disposal. Article 43 of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act

Availability of paid features is verified automatically based on the store-confirmed purchase status, validity period, signatures and security checks. If an entitlement appears incorrect, you may ask customer support for an explanation and review. Where the legal requirements for an automated decision are met, you may exercise the rights provided by law.

Clearing App data or requesting deletion of personal information from the Operator does not cancel a store subscription. If a paid entitlement is reverified later, or if the store sends a new transaction event, a new verification record may be created to the extent there is a lawful basis for processing. Contact the relevant store about accounts and orders it manages. Google Account data and privacy · Apple Data and Privacy

6. Security and Children's Privacy

The Operator uses HTTPS, hashes identifiers in server records, verifies signed entitlements, uses protected device storage and limits server access and request rates. If a breach occurs, the Operator takes the measures and provides the notifications and reports required by applicable law.

The App is not directed at children. Where applicable law requires consent from a child’s legal representative, the Operator verifies that consent. In the Republic of Korea, this applies when consent is required to process the personal information of a child under 14. Legal representatives may contact customer support to exercise the child’s rights.
