Lucky Roulette Analyzer

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

SH Software (Sunghoon Lee, South Korea) is the controller responsible for personal data described in this policy for Lucky Roulette Analyzer on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

1. On-device data

You can use the app without creating an account. The roulette results you enter and the analysis history are processed only on your device. The app may store preferences, an installation identifier and purchase entitlement data on the device.

2. What we process and why

  • Purchases. To verify, restore or recheck a purchase, we process a random installation ID, store, app and product identifiers, request time and type, and purchase proof. Google Play supplies a purchase token and, when required, Play Integrity data. The App Store supplies Apple-signed transaction data. Our server can retain hashed purchase and installation references, verification results, entitlement status and expiration, and store notification records. When paid cosmetics are available, we also process delivery and refund records. We use this information to provide and restore paid features, apply cancellations and refunds, and prevent misuse and duplicate delivery.
  • Operations and security. Technical logs may include IP addresses, user-agent strings, request times and paths, response status and error details. We use them for troubleshooting and service security.
  • Support. We use your email address, messages, attachments and any order details necessary to respond or verify a purchase. Please keep passwords, full card numbers, raw purchase tokens and signed transaction proofs out of support emails.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we use data as needed to perform our contract with you or comply with legal obligations. Where permitted, we use our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and securing the service. We obtain consent where required. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. Service providers and international transfers

The purchase verification service and Firestore database use Seoul, South Korea (asia-northeast3). General operational logs are stored in Seoul for 30 days. Required cloud audit logs are retained for 400 days in a global Google Cloud bucket whose physical storage location is managed by Google. Google Play purchase notifications are stored in Seoul through Cloud Pub/Sub, with unacknowledged messages retained for up to 7 days.

Google Play and Apple handle store accounts, payment details and billing under their own policies. We use encrypted connections to send the information needed for verification to each store.

  • Google Cloud. We use it for verification, database storage, operational logs and delivery of Google Play purchase notices through Cloud Pub/Sub. Data is sent when a verification request or notice is processed or when a log is generated. Retention is described below. Google Cloud contact · Subprocessors and processing locations
  • Google Play. At purchase, restoration or re-verification, we send app identifiers and purchase tokens to Google LLC (United States). If an integrity check is needed, we send the app identifier and Play Integrity token. Google privacy policy · Google privacy contact
  • Apple App Store. We send transaction references to Apple when verifying purchases, restoration, entitlement status or related notifications. The contracting Apple entity varies by storefront. Apple Services Pte. Ltd. (Singapore) operates the South Korean storefront. Apple Inc. (United States) and other Apple affiliates may process data under Apple's policy. Each store applies its own retention rules. Apple privacy policy · Apple privacy contact
  • Gmail. We use Google LLC’s Gmail service for support. When we receive or reply to an email, the address, message, attachments and transmission information may be processed on Google servers worldwide. Our retention criteria are described below.

These services may process data outside your country. Where required, we use a lawful transfer mechanism, such as an applicable adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. You can contact us for details or a copy of the applicable safeguards, or to object to a transfer where the law gives you that right. If a transfer needed for purchase verification or email support cannot occur, that service may be limited. For South Korea, contract-related overseas processing and storage are subject to the requirements of Article 28-8 of the Personal Information Protection Act.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Purchases and notices. Subscription verification records become eligible for automatic deletion 90 days after creation. Store notice records become eligible 90 days after receipt. Rechecking a subscription does not extend its original deadline. Actual deletion may occur later.
  • Paid cosmetics, where offered. Purchase reservations become eligible for automatic deletion 90 days after creation. Detailed delivery records become eligible 90 days after the later of the record update and benefit expiration. We keep a separate transaction hash and fulfillment flag for App Store cosmetics as long as necessary to prevent duplicate delivery.
  • Support and legal records. We keep support correspondence through resolution and any necessary follow-up. We retain additional records only as needed for a dispute or legal obligation. When South Korean statutory retention rules apply to records we hold, we keep contract, withdrawal, payment and supply records for five years and complaint or dispute records for three years. We keep legally required records separately and securely dispose of records once the purpose and applicable retention period end.

5. Your choices and rights

You or your authorized representative can contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of consent, as applicable. We may request the store, product, purchase date or order reference to verify your request and locate the record. You can request an explanation or a manual review of a purchase verification result. A later verification or a new store event can generate a new record when we have a lawful basis to process it.

Right to object and complain. If applicable law gives you these rights, including in the EEA or UK, you can object to processing, request data portability and complain to your local privacy authority. You may also exercise any other privacy rights available under local law.

Data deletion and subscription cancellation are separate requests. Manage subscriptions and requests concerning store-held account and order data directly with the relevant store.

6. Security, children and contact

We use HTTPS, hashed identifiers in verification records, protected on-device storage and limited server access. We verify a parent’s or guardian’s consent where applicable law requires it for processing a child’s personal data.

Contact Sunghoon Lee, the privacy contact for SH Software, at digitalspaceout.official@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests. We publish material updates and their effective date and give further notice or obtain consent where required by law.
